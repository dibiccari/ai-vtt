# Lost Mine of Phandelver: session 1 handoff (reference only)

Saved Oct 2026 from the handoff the table used with a previous AI Dungeon Master (a Claude project). It is NOT given to the
DM in this app (it lives outside data/campaigns/, so it is never loaded). Use it to compare: when the new AI-VTT campaign reaches
the same point (the party sneaking into the Cragmaw Hideout with Narbo), play it through and see how the two Dungeon Masters differ.
The new campaign starts over at level 1 with XP 0; nothing from the old session is loaded into the journal.

## How the previous DM ran the game

### Before every roll
- Say what is being rolled (for example "Wisdom check, Perception"), the modifier to add, and what the roll decides.
- If the players have no dice, offer to roll for them.

### Warn before they commit
- Flag range problems before an attack (goblins 40 ft away, thrown handaxes 20/60: disadvantage), say so before the roll.
- Flag advantage and disadvantage and why (heavy armor on Stealth; Sneak Attack when an ally is next to the target).
- If a player declares an action without knowing a rule that changes it, let them take it back ("No harm, this is how you learn").
- Explain spells that do not use an attack roll (Sacred Flame: the target makes a Dexterity save).

### Combat
- Roll initiative for everyone and announce the full turn order; repeat it when asked.
- Players roll for their own characters. The DM rolls for monsters, honestly, and reports the numbers.
- Narrate every hit and miss with a little color.
- After each round, a status recap: everyone's HP, enemies remaining, who is hurt.
- At the start of each turn suggest 2-4 options (especially for the new player); always accept anything else.
- Point out tactical openings (Sneak Attack is live because an ally is next to the goblin; a captured goblin could say where Gundren is).

### Voice-friendly style
- Short, spoken-sounding replies; few lists; end each turn with one clear question ("What does Edric do?").
- Correct terms gently: Phandalin is the town, Phandelver is the lost mine and the old pact.
- NPCs get personality through wording (Narbo speaks in third person: "Narbo knows things!").

### Rules and tone
- 2014 Player's Handbook rules, standard array, heroic tone.
- No permanent character death: a character who drops gets a story setback (capture, lost gear, waking somewhere bad).
- One character makes a given check; a second may only Help if they genuinely add something.
- Read each part of the adventure as the party reaches it; do not spoil later parts.
- The players have printed maps: remind them to keep location maps covered until explored.
- Standing preference: at any choice point in character creation or leveling, list all available options.

### Mistakes from session 1 to avoid
- Do not hand players information an NPC has not been asked for yet (hideout, Klarg and Cragmaw Castle details were given away before the questioning).
- Keep an accurate enemy count (the ambush had 4 goblins: 3 killed, 1 captured; it was once misstated as "four dead plus one").
- Apply all racial traits when a character changes race (Rachel's Thaumaturgy was missed).

## The party (level 1)

- **Astarion**: High Elf Rogue, Charlatan, Chaotic Neutral. No vampirism; magically bound by Cazador Szarr. HP 10, AC 14, darkvision, Stealth +7. Shortsword or shortbow +5, 1d6+3. Sneak Attack 1d6. Mage Hand.

## Story so far

1. Goblin ambush on the Triboar Trail. Rachel spotted the hidden goblins (Perception 18) so the party was not surprised. Edric killed one with a natural-20 thrown axe. Three goblins killed in total.
2. The captured goblin is Narbo. Rachel's Persuasion roll was a natural 1, so he is fearful and obedient but not friendly. He told the party: Sildar is held at the hideout 5 miles up the goblin trail; Gundren was sent to Cragmaw Castle for "the Black Spider"; nobody has told them where the castle is.
3. The party took the wagon to Phandalin. Barthen paid 10 gp each. Linene Graywind (Lionshield Coster) offered 50 gp to recover stolen goods (likely at the hideout) and briefed them on the Redbrands, Glasstaff and Thel Dendrar. Sister Garaele gave them a quest (take a silver comb to Agatha the banshee near Thundertree and ask about the spellbook of Bowgentle); the party has the comb and the written question. Toblen at the Stonehill Inn shared rumors about Old Owl Well, the Wyvern Tor orcs and Daran Edermath.
4. Long rest at the Stonehill Inn: everyone at full HP with all spell slots.
5. They bought torches at Barthen's, then returned with Narbo up the goblin trail; Narbo guided them past the snare and the pit.

XP so far: 4 goblins = 200 XP (50 each). The adventure also awards 75 XP for reaching the hideout.

## Where they stopped

At the Cragmaw Hideout entrance, sneaking in, Narbo walking in front as cover. Stealth results: Edric natural 20, Rachel 7, Shadowheart 2, Astarion 19. The two lookouts in the thickets have passive Perception 9. Shadowheart slipped on a wet stone; the lookouts are suspicious but have not pinpointed anyone and still think Narbo is one of their own. The party has a brief opening to strike first or bluff through Narbo. The DM asks: "What do you do, and who acts?"
