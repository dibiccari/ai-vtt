Place settings

Turn on the grid, then change this until the grid lines up with the squares printed on the map. One square is 5 ft.

A mood sets the light level and the ambient sound together; you can still change either one above afterwards.

Give the day and night maps of one place the same group name and different version names. A Day/Night switch then appears in the top left of the map.

DM and player versions

Every map has two faces. Player is the picture the table shows. DM is the same map with the pins, the start area, area numbers, secret and locked doors and terrain marked, and it is what the AI DM is shown. Use the DM | Player switch above the map to look at each. "Make the DM version" draws the DM face from this map's set-up and saves it for the DM.

Market

Save this map, with its walls, doors, lights, pins, terrain and mood, to the Market, where any campaign can add it. Pictures from a purchase or from Wizards of the Coast cannot be saved.

Doors

Each door can be normal, locked (the table will not open it until the DM unlocks it; set the DC of the lock) or secret (a plain wall to everyone until the DM reveals it; set the DC to spot it). Orange dots mark locked doors on the map, purple ones secret doors.

Pins

Use the Pins tool. The green ring and circle is where the party starts (drag it, or set its circle size on its card); the blue pins are places the DM can narrate and send the party to. Click empty ground to add a pin (it opens a card to name it), click a pin to rename it, add a note for the DM or remove it, or drag a pin to move it (all pins show while this tool is selected). Every battle map needs a pin named "start" (npm run check fails without one). Other pins are places the AI can send the party to, such as a back door; the AI is told their names. Saved with the map, so the players never need to see it to place themselves.

Module areas: drag a number onto the map to drop its pin (a ✓ means it is placed; drag it again to move it, or right-click the pin to remove it). The AI reads these to know where each area is.
