Campaigns

The active campaign is what the AI Dungeon Master runs. Its text is sent to the DM with every message, so the narration, NPCs, locations and secrets all come from it.

How the DM uses a campaign

1. Campaign textEvery document of the active campaign is sent as the DM's reference. It is cached, so after the first message it costs a fraction.
2. Rules for the DMIt runs the module as secret DM material: no spoilers, it keeps track of where the party is, and uses the module's names.
3. The tableEach message also carries the map name, the tokens and walls, and the character sheets.
4. VoicesNamed NPCs from the campaign get their own voice and acting direction.
