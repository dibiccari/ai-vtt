A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a floor plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: walls are flat bands of grey stone blocks seen from above, towers are flat rings of stone, trees are only round leafy crowns seen from above with soft shadows beside them. Landscape 3:2 picture of a ruined, goblin-held castle in a forest clearing on a 30 by 20 square layout, in bright daylight. The castle fills the middle of the picture, about 22 squares wide and 15 squares tall: a thick outer curtain wall of grey stone blocks, partly crumbled with a few gaps and rubble piles, and FIVE round stone towers (flat rings of thick stone with a floor inside): one at each of the four corners and one larger tower at the north-east, plus a sixth small round tower on the south side. The main gate is a gap in the west wall with a broad stone stair going down to the outside. Inside the walls: an open flagstone courtyard in the west; in the middle a long banquet hall with two long wooden tables; to the north a round chamber with a goblin shrine; a ruined barracks with broken bunks and a storeroom with crates and barrels in the north-west; a hobgoblin barracks with beds in the east; the biggest, north-east tower holds a king's quarters with a large bed and a throne. Short interior stone walls and doorways (gaps with wooden doors) divide the rooms. Collapsed roofs show as heaps of broken timber and rubble here and there. Around the castle: meadow grass, a worn dirt trail winding in from the west edge to the gate, two curved earth embankments, big round tree crowns along every edge, scattered boulders.

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy natural colours (fresh grass greens, warm brown earth, grey and tan stone, warm sandy floors), gentle drop shadows under walls, towers, boulders and trees, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
