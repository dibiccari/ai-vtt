A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a landscape plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: trees are shown only as round leafy crowns seen from above with soft shadows beside them, bushes and thickets as flat clumps of foliage, boulders as flat grey rounded stones, and earth embankments as flat bands of bare brown earth and exposed roots with a dark shaded edge. Landscape 3:2 picture of a wooded roadside in daylight. ONE single wide worn dirt trail with scattered cobbles and small stones winds across the whole picture as one continuous ribbon about three squares wide, from the middle of the west (left) edge, in a gentle S-curve, to the east (right) edge slightly lower down; the trail never splits or forms loops. A steep earth embankment (a long curved band of bare earth and roots with a shaded drop) runs along the NORTH side of the trail, and a second one runs along the SOUTH side, each separated from the trail by a strip of grass, each with a few gaps. Dense thickets of bushes, big enough to hide a creature, grow at the foot of both embankments: two clumps on each side of the trail in the eastern half. Scattered grey boulders and a fallen log near the trail. Large round tree crowns fill the four corners. Meadow grass everywhere else. IMPORTANT: the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy natural colours (fresh grass greens, warm brown earth and trail, grey stones), gentle drop shadows under bushes, boulders and trees, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.
