A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a floor plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: tables, beds, crates, barrels, coffins and chairs are flat shapes seen from above, a cistern is a flat rounded rectangle of water, stairs are flat stripes of steps, walls are flat bands of stone seen from above. Landscape 3:2 picture of the Redbrand Hideout, a torch-lit dungeon of cellars and crypts under a ruined manor, on a 30 by 20 square layout. Everything outside the dungeon is flat solid black. Every room has an EVENLY lit, light grey-tan flagstone floor (a bright, clearly readable floor with no dark vignette and no heavy shadow), surrounded by thick walls of darker grey dressed stone blocks with a thin dark ink outline, so floor and walls contrast clearly. Doorways are plain gaps in the walls with a thin dark wooden door frame (no door leaf). Layout: WEST block (left third of the picture): three rooms stacked from north to south, joined by short corridors: in the north a chamber with a red rug, a four-poster bed and a desk (the wizard's quarters) beside a smaller room with a long table of glass flasks (a workshop); in the middle a big common room with a large round table, chairs and a stove; in the south a barracks with six bunk beds and a footlocker. In the MIDDLE, running north to south, a deep rocky crevasse: a jagged ravine of dark rock with narrow stone ledges on both sides and two rope-and-plank bridges across it. EAST block (right two thirds): in the north-middle a storeroom and an armory with weapon racks; to their east a crypt room with four stone sarcophagi and a row of cells with iron bars (slave pens); along the middle a long corridor with a red carpet (a trapped hall); in the south-east a big cellar with a large stone cistern full of green water, stacked barrels and crates along the walls, a short staircase going down, and a small barracks with bedrolls beside it; a door and a stair lead to the east edge of the picture. Warm torch sconces on the walls glow softly but do not darken anything.

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy colours (grey-tan flagstone, warm wood, dark red rugs, green water), gentle drop shadows under furniture, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the floor is plain painted flagstone with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
