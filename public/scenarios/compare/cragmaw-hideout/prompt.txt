A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a floor plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: boulders are flat grey rounded stones, crates and tents are flat shapes seen from above, a wooden bridge is a flat strip of planks. Landscape 3:2 picture of a goblin cave hideout beside a stream on a 30 by 20 square layout. The walkable cave floor is a bright warm sandy-tan packed dirt with scattered pebbles, in winding passages and caverns; ALL solid rock is painted as a flat, uniform, very dark charcoal black (almost pure black, no texture, no ridges, no shading) so that the floor stands out brightly against it, with a thin dark ink outline and a narrow fringe of loose rubble along the edge of every passage. Layout: in the north-west a wide cavern (the goblin den) with a few small ragged tents, bedrolls and a firepit; from it a passage runs east along the top, past two black rock pillars standing in the passage, to a wooden plank bridge crossing a stream near the top middle. The stream is turquoise water; it begins in a small pool with a waterfall in the north-east corner, runs south-west under the bridge, then runs straight south through the middle of the picture to the bottom edge, shallow enough to wade, with a few stepping stones. In the middle a narrow steep winding passage leads down south, with a kennel pen of crude wooden fences and a cage. In the south-east a large cavern (the chieftain's cave) with a campfire, crates, barrels, a table and a pile of furs. In the south-west corner, OUTSIDE the cave, an open forest meadow with green grass and big round tree crowns, where the cave mouth opens: a wide gap in the rock at the bottom middle-left, screened by thick briar bushes. Several black rock pillars and boulders stand inside the caverns as obstacles. Warm torchless even daylight-like lighting inside the cave (no dark vignette, every floor area evenly bright).

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy colours (sandy tan floor, turquoise water, forest greens, grey stones, warm wood), gentle drop shadows under boulders and objects, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
