A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a landscape plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: trees are only round leafy crowns seen from above with soft shadows beside them, boulders are flat grey rounded stones, ruined walls are flat bands of crumbling grey stone blocks, a tent is a flat octagon of canvas. Landscape 3:2 picture of the ruins of Old Owl Well, an ancient watchtower on a rugged hilltop, on a 30 by 20 square layout, in bright daylight. A rocky hilltop plateau fills the middle, with rough cliff edges of piled grey boulders curving all around it, and dry scrubby grass and bare tan earth outside. On the plateau: the broken stump of a round watchtower in the north-centre, a flat thick ring of crumbled stone about six squares across with a rubble-filled dirt floor and a half-collapsed spiral stair around its inside; next to it a courtyard enclosed by low, crumbling wall mounds that only reach waist height, broken into several short rubble wall pieces with wide gaps, forming two small ruined rooms on the west side and two on the east side; in the very centre an old round stone well with a rim of stone and clear water; just west of the well a colourful teal-green octagonal canvas tent with guy ropes. Dirt footpaths wind up the slopes from the south and the west edges onto the plateau. Scattered boulders, a few small scrubby trees, clumps of grass. 

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy natural colours (dry grass greens, warm tan earth, grey stone, a teal tent), gentle drop shadows under walls, boulders and trees, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
