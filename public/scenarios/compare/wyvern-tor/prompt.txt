A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a landscape plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: cliffs are shown as flat wide bands of piled grey rock with a dark shaded edge, boulders as flat grey rounded stones, trees only as round leafy crowns seen from above with soft shadows, tents as flat canvas shapes. SQUARE picture of the rugged lower slopes of Wyvern Tor, a hill in wild country, on a 30 by 30 square layout, in bright daylight. Dry grass and bare tan earth with rocky outcrops. A turquoise stream with a small waterfall flows from the top middle, running south-west through a narrow ravine and then curving west across the lower half, shallow enough to wade. Steep ridges of piled grey rock run across the picture, one long curved ridge along the north-west side with the black mouth of a cave opening in it at the upper left, and several large rounded rocky hills (flat plateaus of stacked rock with grassy tops) on the east and south-west sides. Worn dirt trails wind along the ridges. In the upper left, just outside the cave mouth, a few ragged orc tents, a smoking firepit and some crude wooden stakes in the dirt. A big boulder stands twenty yards from the cave mouth. Scattered boulders, scrubby bushes and a few big round tree crowns.

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy natural colours (dry grass greens, warm tan earth, grey rock, turquoise water), gentle drop shadows under rocks, boulders and trees, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
