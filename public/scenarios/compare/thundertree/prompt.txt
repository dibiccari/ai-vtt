A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a landscape plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: ruined buildings are flat outlines of crumbling grey stone walls seen from above (a thin band of stone blocks around a floor of dirt, broken boards and rubble, with gaps where walls have fallen), a round tower is a flat thick ring of stone, trees are only round leafy crowns seen from above with soft shadows beside them. Landscape 3:2 picture of the overgrown ruined village of Thundertree, destroyed long ago by a volcano and now reclaimed by the forest, on a 30 by 20 square layout, in bright daylight. About a dozen ruined stone cottages and shops of different sizes stand scattered in a loose ring around an open cobbled town square in the middle with an old toppled statue plinth; every building is roofless, with broken walls, a doorway gap, heaps of rubble and weeds growing inside. In the north-centre the broken round stump of a ruined stone tower (a thick flat ring of stone with a spiral stair inside) stands next to a ruined hall; in the north-east a larger ruined garrison with several rooms; in the south-centre an old smithy with a cold forge and anvil; in the south-west a farmhouse with a collapsed barn; in the east a herbalist's shop with toppled shelves. Old overgrown cobbled lanes and dirt paths wind between the buildings from the west edge. Vines, ivy, bushes and fallen logs creep over everything. Thick dark forest tree crowns crowd all four edges and corners and a few stand between the buildings.

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy natural colours (mossy greens, warm tan cobbles, grey stone), gentle drop shadows under walls, rubble and trees, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
