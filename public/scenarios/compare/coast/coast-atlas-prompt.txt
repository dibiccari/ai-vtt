A hand-illustrated fantasy REGIONAL MAP of a wild frontier coast, in PORTRAIT orientation, seen from directly above like an atlas page. The cold sea fills a strip along the whole west (left) edge, with a long, gently curving coastline running from the top-left corner down to the bottom-centre and a few wave lines in the water. A long road follows the coast from the top-left, passes a large walled port city at a river mouth about halfway down the left side, and continues south-east along the coast to a small ruined town near the bottom, ending beside a great reedy marsh at the bottom edge. East of the port city a winding river flows eastward into a vast ancient forest that fills the centre and north of the map: its northern part has a lone smoking volcanic mountain on its western side with rocky hills around it and a pair of rivers winding through the trees; its southern lobe reaches down the middle of the map and holds a ruined castle. North of the forest are rugged crags and rocky hills. Along the east (right) edge: a ridge of rocky hills in the north-east, and rugged mountains in the south-east with one tall lone snow-capped peak, a cave in the range, and a smaller dense forest in the far south-east corner. In the south-centre are open green meadows and grassland with a small frontier town in the foothills; a dotted trail runs from the coast road north-eastwards across the grassland, past the forest edge, to the east edge, passing two small ruined sites and a hidden lair. A few more ruins and points of interest are scattered about. Overhead view only, no perspective.
