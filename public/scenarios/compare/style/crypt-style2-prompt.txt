A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a floor plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: round pillars appear as flat circles, stone tombs and altars and tables and crates as flat rectangles, barrels as circles, the staircase as flat stripes of steps, and walls as flat bands of stone seen from above. Landscape 3:2 picture of a small torch-lit crypt beneath a ruined chapel on a 30 by 20 square layout. Four stone-floored rooms joined by short narrow corridors with wooden double doors: an entry hall in the north-west with a stone staircase in one corner and two round pillars; a large pillared central chamber in the north with a stone altar and a glowing brazier; a sarcophagus crypt in the south-west with four stone tombs; and a wooden-floored storeroom in the south-east with crates, barrels and a chest. Thick dark stone walls clearly outlined around every room and corridor, black empty rock outside the walls, flagstone floors, warm torch light. No grid lines, no text, no letters, no people. 
Art style: a crisp, high-contrast, digitally painted and ink-outlined tabletop battle map in the tradition of published adventure map packs. Clean flat-to-lightly-textured colour fills: warm tan and sand flagstone floors with subtle mottling, cool grey stone, blue water, brown wood planks. Bold black ink outlines around every wall, room and object, a little heavier on the outer walls. Wall bands rendered as clean masonry with ink-drawn stone blocks and soft cross-hatched shadow on the shaded side. Doors as clean brown wooden rectangles with dark outlines and small handles. Rubble, boulders and bones as small ink-outlined stones with hatch shading. Stairs as crisp parallel steps with a shadow edge. Furniture, tombs, crates and barrels as simple, readable shapes with a thin dark outline and light highlights. Even, gentle lighting with only a little torch glow, not heavy darkness: clear separation between floor and wall, uncluttered and very easy to read at table scale. No grid lines, no text.
