A top-down, hand-illustrated fantasy REGIONAL MAP of a gentle, peaceful pastoral countryside in the spirit of a cosy farming land: rolling green hills with soft hill-shading, a patchwork of small farm fields divided by hedgerows, orchards and vegetable plots, a winding river with a small mill and a stone bridge, a little lake, scattered tiny villages made of small cottages and round-doored homes dug into grassy hills, winding dirt roads that join the villages, a main road crossing the river on the bridge, small woods and copses, a larger old forest along the west edge and a distant ridge of blue-grey mountains along the east edge. Warm parchment-toned paper, ink outlines with soft watercolour washes, seen from directly above like an overland travel map. No text, no letters, no labels, no compass, no border, no grid, no people. Landscape orientation, 3:2. 
Art style: a richly detailed painted-and-inked fantasy atlas map in the tradition of published campaign-setting maps. Crisp, confident black ink outlines around every coastline, forest edge and ridge. Dense forests drawn as tightly packed rounded, scalloped tree crowns in saturated deep teal-green with dark outlines and soft lighter highlights, so a forest reads as one bold dark-green mass with a lobed edge. Open lowlands and farmland in pale yellow-green with fine hand-inked texture, tiny hatching and stippling. Hills as small shaded mounds. Mountain ranges as crowded, ink-hatched ridges in warm grey, ochre and sepia with cross-hatched shadow sides and pale lit sides. Rivers in clear blue with thin dark outlines, widening toward the sea. Seas and lakes in turquoise with long, flowing hand-drawn wave lines that follow the coastlines. A few thin red roads and small black dots for settlements. Aged parchment feel with darker mottled, stained edges and a soft vignette. Clean, high-contrast, saturated but balanced colour, readable at a glance. No text, no labels, no compass, no scale bar, no border, no grid.
