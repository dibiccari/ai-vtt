A top-down, hand-illustrated fantasy REGIONAL MAP of a rugged frontier coast: a cold sea along the west edge with a ragged shoreline, cliffs and a few rocky islets; windswept grassy hills and moorland; dark pine forests in the south and east; a range of snow-capped mountains across the north; a long dirt trail running north to south along the coast and branching inland; a small walled harbour town on a bay, two tiny hamlets, a ruined watchtower on a hill, a ruined castle in the north-east foothills, a river that runs from the mountains to the sea with a stone bridge on the trail, and a small lake. Warm parchment-toned paper, ink outlines with soft watercolour washes, seen from directly above like an overland travel map. No text, no letters, no labels, no compass, no border, no grid, no people. Landscape orientation, 3:2.

Crisp, confident black ink outlines around every coastline, forest edge and ridge. Dense forests drawn as tightly packed rounded, scalloped tree crowns in saturated deep teal-green with dark outlines and soft lighter highlights, so a forest reads as one bold dark-green mass with a lobed edge. Open lowlands and farmland in pale yellow-green with fine hand-inked texture, tiny hatching and stippling. Hills as small shaded mounds. Mountain ranges as crowded, ink-hatched ridges in warm grey, ochre and sepia with cross-hatched shadow sides and pale lit sides. Rivers in clear blue with thin dark outlines, widening toward the sea. Seas and lakes in turquoise with long, flowing hand-drawn wave lines that follow the coastlines. A few thin red roads and small black dots for settlements. Aged parchment feel with darker mottled, stained edges and a soft vignette. Clean, high-contrast, saturated but balanced colour, readable at a glance.
