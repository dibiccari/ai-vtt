A top-down, hand-illustrated fantasy REGIONAL MAP of a gentle, peaceful pastoral countryside in the spirit of a cosy farming land: rolling green hills with soft hill-shading, a patchwork of small farm fields divided by hedgerows, orchards and vegetable plots, a winding river with a small mill and a stone bridge, a little lake, scattered tiny villages made of small cottages and round-doored homes dug into grassy hills, winding dirt roads that join the villages, a main road crossing the river on the bridge, small woods and copses, a larger old forest along the west edge and a distant ridge of blue-grey mountains along the east edge. Warm parchment-toned paper, ink outlines with soft watercolour washes, seen from directly above like an overland travel map. No text, no letters, no labels, no compass, no border, no grid, no people. Landscape orientation, 3:2. 
Painted in the style of the fantasy cartographer Mike Schley.
