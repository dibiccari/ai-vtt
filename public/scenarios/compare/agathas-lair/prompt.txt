A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a landscape plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: trees are shown only as round leafy crowns seen from above with soft shadows beside them, bushes as flat clumps of foliage, boulders as flat grey rounded stones, furniture as flat shapes seen from above. Landscape 3:2 picture of a dark, still, mossy forest on a 30 by 20 square layout, in cold dim twilight light. In the middle of the picture, in a small clearing, stands a domelike shelter made from the warped branches of trees standing close together, woven into a screen: seen from above it is an irregular rounded footprint about 10 by 8 squares, a ring of thick gnarled woven branches and vines forming its wall, with a wooden plank floor inside and a low doorway gap in the south wall. Inside, sparsely furnished with old elven furniture of fine craft: two chests against the west wall, a shelf along the north wall, a round table with a few items in the middle, and a reclined couch in the north-east corner with a faded rug. A faint cold pale blue glow in the middle of the floor. A narrow worn old trail, mossy and half overgrown, winds in from the south-west corner of the picture to the doorway. Dense trees fill the rest of the picture: large dark round crowns of oak and pine along all edges and in the corners, thick moss, ferns, hanging vine patches, a few mossy boulders and fallen branches, leaving a clearing around the shelter. Cold muted palette: deep teal and moss greens, grey stones, cool shadows, dark brown wood. Hand-painted with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, gentle drop shadows under trees and boulders, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
