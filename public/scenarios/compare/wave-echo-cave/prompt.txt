A STRICTLY OVERHEAD, bird's-eye BATTLE MAP for a tabletop roleplaying game: every object is drawn exactly as seen from directly above with an orthographic camera pointing straight down (like a floor plan painted in detail). No perspective, no side views, no isometric angle, no 3D elevation: boulders are flat grey rounded stones, mine cart rails are flat strips of rails and sleepers, pillars are flat circles, rooms are flat floors with walls of grey stone blocks seen from above. PORTRAIT 2:3 picture of the Wave Echo Cave, a vast lost dwarven mine of winding cave passages, on a 20 wide by 30 tall square layout. The walkable cave floor is a bright warm sandy-tan packed dirt with pebbles, in winding tunnels and caverns; ALL solid rock is painted as a flat, uniform, very dark charcoal black (almost pure black, no texture, no ridges, no shading) so that the floor stands out brightly, with a thin dark ink outline and a narrow fringe of loose rubble along the edge of every passage. The floor is EVENLY bright everywhere: no dark vignette, no heavy shadows. Layout from south (bottom) to north (top): at the bottom-left the cave mouth where a tunnel enters; a network of mine tunnels with wooden mine-cart rails running north; in the south-middle two small stone-walled rooms with grey flagstone floors (an old guardroom and an assayer's office with desks); in the south-east a cavern full of glowing green fungi and mushroom clumps; in the middle a great cavern with the ruined remains of a dwarven bridge and two large rock pillars; to its north-west a stone-walled barracks room with bunks and a dark blue pool of water in a cave on the far west side; north-east of the middle a smelter cavern with a rusty furnace; on the east side a glittering cavern whose floor sparkles with tiny white crystals like a night sky, and beside it a stone-walled wizards' quarters room and a room with a glowing magical forge circle on the floor; in the north-east a huge cavern with a big lake of turquoise water with islands; along the north a dry old streambed winding between rocks; in the north-west a grand temple hall with a grey stone floor, rows of round pillars and an altar. A few boulders stand in the caverns as obstacles.

Art style: hand-painted fantasy battle map with confident dark ink outlines and soft painted shading, warm earthy colours (sandy tan floor, turquoise water, green fungi, grey stone, warm wood), gentle drop shadows under boulders and objects, crisp and readable at tabletop scale.

IMPORTANT (without this the model draws graph paper): the ground is plain painted terrain with absolutely NO grid, NO squares, NO graph paper, NO ruled lines, NO paper texture lines anywhere; no text, no letters, no people, no animals, no tokens.
