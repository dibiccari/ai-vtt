Maps every place

Every place on this server as one entry: a day and night pair is one place, and a DM picture sits with its player picture. For each: its kind and tiles (squares for battle maps, hexagons for regional maps), walls, doors, lights, pins, whether it has a start spot, and whether a DM version of the picture is saved. Open any of them in Map Test, where the DM | Player switch shows each face.
