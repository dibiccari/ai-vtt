The Rusty Flagon: Tavern Brawl

A crowded tavern, eight NPCs and a fight waiting to happen. It exercises social interaction, exploration and combat with real 5e stat blocks.

Your party starts just inside the front door. The barkeep, a bouncer, two regulars, a loud mercenary with his two friends, and a nervous stranger in the back booth are all in the room.

    DM version: where everyone stands and the hidden trap. The AI is shown this same picture on every turn, so it knows where things are. Players never see it.

    30 × 20 squares. Built from rusty-flagon.dd2vtt, a Universal VTT file with its walls, four doors and lights.

    What the AI can change on the board

    Board updateWhat it does
    moveTokenMoves an existing token to a square, for example an NPC running for the door.
    addTokenPuts a new NPC or monster on the map, for example reinforcements bursting in.
    removeTokenTakes a token off the map, for example a creature that fled or was defeated.
    setHpChanges a player character's current hit points. NPC hit points live only in the narration.
    addWallAdds a wall or door segment to the map.

    The AI cannot change the map picture, your character sheets, spell slots or the dice you roll. It is told to refuse moves that are longer than a token's remaining movement or blocked by walls, and to name the 5e rule it uses.
