Character conditions

Tick a condition to put a ring around the token. Each condition has its own colour and line style, and several rings stack outward. An invisible token is drawn faint. The effects box shows what the table does with the same conditions: speed, how far it sees (the room goes dark when sight shrinks), whether it can act, and who gets advantage or disadvantage.
