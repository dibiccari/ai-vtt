Character conditions

Tick a condition to put a ring around the token (the 15 official 5e conditions: each has its own colour and line style, and several rings stack outward). Tick an effect (concentrating, surprised, a spell like sanctuary or bless) to put a small pill with its name beside the token; effects are not conditions. An invisible token is drawn faint. The effects box shows what the table does with the same conditions: speed, how far it sees (the room goes dark when sight shrinks), whether it can act, and who gets advantage or disadvantage.
